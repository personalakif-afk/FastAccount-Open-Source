Invoices: number of invoices, total invoiced, received, outstanding, overdue, and this month’s total.
Bills: the same figures for purchases (total billed, paid, payable, overdue, this month).
Payments: number of payments, received, paid out, and net.
Customers: count, total invoiced, outstanding, and top customer.
Suppliers: count, total billed, payable, and top supplier.
Inventory: items, units in stock, stock value at cost, low stock, and out of stock.
Expenses: entries, total, this month, and top expense account.
Journals: entries, total posted, and latest entry date.
Chart of Accounts: accounts, assets, liabilities, and net profit to date.
Reports: revenue, expenses, net profit, receivables, and cash. These follow the From / To dates you set.
POS: sales today, amount today, average sale, POS total this month, and items in stock.
Users, Business Profile and Settings: user counts, record counts, default tax, currency, and lock date.
